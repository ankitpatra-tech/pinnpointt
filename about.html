Why Direct Sharing

Why Direct Sharing Beats the Cloud

Sending a file to someone sitting next to you shouldn't take a detour through a corporate data center.

Traditional Cloud Sharing
  • Double Waiting: You wait 15 minutes to upload to a cloud server, then your receiver waits another 15 minutes to download.
  • Paywalls & 2 GB Caps: Free cloud services cut you off at 2 GB, throttle download speeds, or demand monthly credit card subscriptions.
  • Files Stored Remotely: Cloud providers store your sensitive documents and personal photos on their remote servers.
  • Ecosystem Silos: AirDrop only connects Apple devices; Quick Share is fragmented across vendor platforms.
PinnPointt Direct Sharing
  • Direct Device-to-Device: Data streams straight from your device to the other device across your Wi-Fi with zero cloud storage.
  • No File Size Limits: Share 50 MB or 50 GB. Smooth streaming ensures your browser never freezes or crashes.
  • 100% Private & Temporary: Nothing is ever saved to any server. Once the transfer finishes, it leaves no trace behind.
  • Universal Device Freedom: Instant compatibility across iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, iPad, and Chromebooks right in the browser.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about PinnPointt.

How large of a file can I send?

There are no file size limits. Whether you are sending a 10 MB document or a 50 GB 4K video collection, PinnPointt streams data smoothly in chunks so your browser never runs out of memory or crashes.

Do my files ever go to a cloud server?

Never. Your files travel straight from one device to the other over your local Wi-Fi or direct peer-to-peer connection. No server stores your data, and when the transfer completes, no traces are left anywhere.

Can I send between iPhone and Windows?

Yes. PinnPointt works seamlessly across all platforms. You can share files between iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, and Chromebooks without installing any special software.

What happens if the transfer gets paused or interrupted?

If you pause or the connection temporarily drops, PinnPointt remembers the exact byte offset where it paused and resumes right where it left off, so you never have to re-upload from 0%.

Under The Hood

Curious how it works under the hood? Read the technical summary

4-Channel Parallel WebRTC SCTP Pool

Transfers are multiplexed across four concurrent SCTP DataChannels with round-robin chunk dispatching based on low-watermark buffer backpressure to saturate local Wi-Fi speeds.

Direct-to-Disk Stream Handling

Chunks stream directly into Origin Private File System handles using FileSystemWritableFileStream, eliminating browser tab RAM spikes and crash risks on multi-gigabyte transfers.

WebRTC Transport Encryption

Direct peer connections are cryptographically authenticated and encrypted using DTLS 1.3 with AES-GCM-256 ciphers. Zero unencrypted packets ever traverse the network.

Hybrid Serverless Signaling Relay

When WebSocket signaling is blocked, peers can bootstrap SDP offers and ICE candidates through decentralized, ephemeral Nostr relays (NIP-01) with zero server persistence.

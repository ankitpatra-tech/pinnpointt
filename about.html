Problem vs Solution

Why PinnPointt Exists

Sending a file to someone sitting next to you shouldn't take a detour through a corporate data center. PinnPointt fixes everyday file sharing once and for all.

The Traditional Cloud Way
  • ✕ The Data Center Detour: Sending a simple video uploads gigabytes to third-party cloud servers hundreds of miles away, only for the receiver to download it again.
  • ✕ Paywalls & 2 GB Caps: Free services cut you off at 2 GB, throttle download speeds, or demand monthly credit card subscriptions.
  • ✕ Your Files Are Stored Remotely: Cloud providers store your sensitive documents and personal photos on their remote disks.
  • ✕ Walled Ecosystems: AirDrop only connects Apple devices; Quick Share is fragmented across Android and Windows.
The PinnPointt Advantage
  • ✓ Direct Device-to-Device: Data flows straight from your device to the other device across your local Wi-Fi or direct peer internet with zero cloud storage.
  • ✓ No File Size Limits: Share 50 MB or 50 GB. PinnPointt streams data in smooth chunks so your browser never freezes or crashes.
  • ✓ 100% Private & Temporary: Nothing is ever saved to any server. Once the transfer finishes, the connection closes and leaves no trace behind.
  • ✓ Universal Device Compatibility: Works instantly on iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, and Chromebooks right in the browser.

How PinnPointt Detects and Defends Against Malicious Content

A multi-layered client-side threat triage shield protects users without compromising zero-knowledge privacy:

01

Client-Side Executable Quarantine

Executable and script formats (.exe, .scr, .bat, .vbs, .ps1, .apk) are automatically flagged with warning badges and quarantined in browser memory. They are never auto-downloaded or auto-executed.

02

Double-Extension Trap Detection

Malicious deception techniques like invoice.pdf.exe or photo.jpg.scr are detected immediately before transmission, alerting both sender and recipient to the high-risk disguise.

03

Pre-Transfer Hash Signature Check

The file's SHA-256 digest is matched locally against known threat signatures. Known malicious payloads are blocked client-side without sending files to external cloud antivirus scanners.

04

Zero Cloud Storage / Zero Distribution Vector

Because PinnPointt has zero server storage, no public URLs, and strictly limits rooms to 2 peers with a 2-minute auto-expiry, it is architecturally impossible to use as a persistent malware warehouse or distribution channel.

Hey everyone,

I built PinnPointt because sharing a file between two devices in 2026 is still surprisingly frustrating.

Think about what usually happens: you want to send a video from your phone to your laptop, or share photos with a friend sitting next to you who has a different phone. You're forced to upload it to a cloud drive, wait forever, hit a 2 GB storage limit, or sign up for an account you didn't want. I wanted something that just works: open the page, point your camera, and send the file straight across at full speed. No logins, no cloud storage, and no size limits.

PinnPointt is completely free, does not track you, and will become 100% open-source under the MIT license when we reach our v1.0.0 release so the community owns it forever.

Thank you for using PinnPointt and helping make the web faster and more private for all of us.
